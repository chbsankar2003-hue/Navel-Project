The Navel SDK is provided/configured on the robot according to the lab setup.

For the initial project, do not install a guessed `navel` package on your home laptop.
Use SSH to run these scripts on the robot:
    ssh navel@ROBOT_IP
    cd ~/control-studio/scripts/room_navigator
    python3 01_inspect_sensors.py

The lab PC may be Ubuntu or Windows. It only needs an SSH client for this workflow.
